Photo Measure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Photo Measure ("the app") is a job photo and measurement app for Android, made by Filling Gaps, Australia.

We don't collect any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos and measurements are processed only on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any server of ours.

  • Your data stays on your phone. Photos you take or import, and the jobs, addresses, rooms, measurements, notes, materials and prices you enter, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or deleting a job or photo in the app, removes that data from the app's storage.
  • Device backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. That is controlled by you and Google, and we cannot access it.
  • Permissions. Camera is used to take job photos, panoramas and close-ups. Photos or media access is used only for pictures you choose to import. Bluetooth ("Nearby devices") is used only to connect to a laser measure you choose; the app does not use Bluetooth to track you. On Android 11 and older, Android also requires a location permission for Bluetooth scanning; Photo Measure does not use or store your location. The motion sensor is used to guide you while taking a panorama. The app does not use the microphone and does not record sound.
  • Sharing and backups. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it. When you share a PDF, web page, photos, a materials list or a backup file, the app creates that file on your device and opens Android's share sheet. It goes only to the app or person you pick (for example email, Google Drive or a messaging app), under that service's own privacy policy. Backup files you create are yours to keep or delete.
  • Purchases. The optional Pro upgrade (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Internet. The app works fully offline. It connects only to Google Play (to check or make the Pro purchase), and opens your share sheet or browser if you choose to share something or tap an external link.
  • Children. The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
