Privacy Policy: Fair Go Rotations

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Fair Go Rotations ("the app") is made by Filling Gaps, Australia.

What we collect: Nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your team data: Player names, position preferences, games and season tallies that you enter are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not included in Android cloud backups. Uninstalling the app, or tapping More → "Delete all data on this phone", deletes them permanently.

Sharing: When you tap Share, the app creates an image or PDF of your rotation on your device and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only to the app or person you choose.

Purchases: The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to that transaction: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children: The app is intended for adult coaches and parents. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
