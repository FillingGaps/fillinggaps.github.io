Splashwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Splashwise ("the app") is a pool chemistry calculator for Android.

We don't collect any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Your data stays on your phone. Pool details and test readings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.
  • Device backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. That is controlled by you and Google, and we cannot access it.
  • Purchases. If you buy Splashwise Pro, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Internet. The app works fully offline. It connects only to Google Play (to check or make the Pro purchase), and opens your web browser if you tap an external link.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
